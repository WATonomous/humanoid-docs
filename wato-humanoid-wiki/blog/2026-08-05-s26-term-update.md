As the term wraps up, here's where the humanoid team landed this summer — and it's been a big one.

{/* truncate */}

## This term

- **40+ contributing members** — the team grew to one of the largest design teams at UWaterloo, all in a single term.
- **A functional 7 DOF humanoid arm** — currently working through a motor issue, fix incoming.
- **A 22 DOF humanoid hand** with fully routed tendons.
- **A fully designed 12 DOF humanoid leg**, set to be built next term.
- A full-body humanoid is coming soon, and everything above already exists in simulation for ML training.
- Renewed partnerships and sponsorship — companies like CubeMars, plus a growing list of people reaching out to learn more about the team.

We set our goals for the term in our [quest book](https://wiki.watonomous.ca/quest_books/s26_humanoid_quests/) at the start of the summer, and we've far exceeded all of them.

## Where we're headed

One-year goal: a humanoid that walks around campus on its own, with arms doing useful work — assembling its own components, folding clothes, that kind of thing.
