# Simulation

## Fin Ray Gripper

Fin ray fingers bend around what they grip instead of pushing it away, so the grasp wraps the object's shape. This cross-section shows the two fingers bending around a cylinder as they close.

![](/img/humanoid/videos/finray-gripper-cylinder-squeeze.mp4)

## Deformable Object Physics

A soft deformable block like a sponge, grasped, lifted, and held. The point here is the object deforming, not the gripper, which can be modeled as a rigid body to keep the sim simple.

![](/img/humanoid/videos/deformable-object-grasp.mp4)
